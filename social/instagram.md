## Post · How does a watch tick? (out/watch-majestic.mp4)
How does a watch tick? 🎧⌚
Turn the sound on: every tick you hear is a tick you see.

A wound spring pushes the gears… but a tiny fork only lets the escape wheel slip half a tooth at a time. A balance wheel swings back and forth, kicking the fork, and a hair-thin spring makes every swing take exactly the same time.

⚙️ 8 ticks every second
⚙️ 691,200 ticks a day
⚙️ over 250 million a year
…all from one tiny spring.

## Post · How do bees build perfect hexagons? (out/bees-pixel.mp4)
How do bees build perfect hexagons? 🐝🎧
No plan. No tools. No boss.

Young bees hang in chains and make wax from 8 glands under their belly. They chew it soft and press it into walls, cell by cell.

Why hexagons? Circles leave gaps. Squares use more wall. Hexagons leave no gaps AND use the least wax (~7% less than squares) — and wax costs bees about 8 kg of honey per kg.
Mathematicians only proved it in 1999. Bees knew all along.

## Post · How does fusion power the Sun? (out/fusion.mp4)
How does fusion power the Sun? ☀️🎧
The Sun isn't on fire. Its core is 15 million °C, so hot that atoms fall apart into bare protons.

Protons repel each other… but it's so hot and crowded they slam together and fuse: deuterium, then helium-3, then helium-4. 4 protons in, 1 helium out, and the helium is 0.7% lighter.
That missing mass becomes energy. E = mc².

☀️ 600 million tonnes of hydrogen fused every second
☀️ 4 million tonnes turned into pure energy every second
☀️ that light takes thousands of years to get out… and 8 minutes to reach you

## Post · How does your DNA copy itself? (out/dna.mp4)
How does your DNA copy itself? 🧬🎧
Every cell holds 2 meters of DNA, packed into a nucleus ~6 µm wide. It's a code with just 4 letters: A always pairs with T, C with G.

To copy it, a helicase unzips the helix, and a polymerase grabs free letters and matches every single one. Each copy ends up with 1 old strand + 1 new strand, and it even proofreads itself: about 1 mistake per billion letters.

🧬 ~50 letters per second
🧬 at thousands of forks at once
🧬 ~330 billion new cells in your body every day, every one copied like this

## Post · How do screens work? (out/screens.mp4)
How does your screen make every color? 📱🎧
Zoom in far enough and it's all pixels. Each pixel is 3 tiny lights: red, green and blue.
Red + green = yellow. Green + blue = cyan. All three = white.
Each light has 256 brightness levels → 16.7 million colors, and your phone has over 2 million pixels, redrawn 60–120 times a second. Your eye blends it all into one picture.

Sound on 🔊 the pixel IS the drum machine: red = kick, green = snare, blue = hats.

## Post · How does a baby form? (out/baby.mp4)
How does a baby form? 👶🎧
~250 million sperm set off. A few hundred reach the egg. Only ONE gets in.
That single cell splits 1 → 2 → 4 → 8… and by day 5 it's a hollow ball of ~100 cells that settles into the womb.

🫀 week 6: a heartbeat (~140 beats a minute, the tempo of this song)
🍓 week 8: ~1.6 cm, a raspberry
🍋 week 12: ~5 cm, a lime
🍌 week 20: ~25 cm, a banana
🍉 week 40: ~50 cm, ~3.4 kg, head down and ready

It all started as one cell.
