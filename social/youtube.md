## What does LSD actually do to your brain? (out/lsd.mp4)
Title: What LSD actually does to your brain 🧠🤯 #shorts
Description:
A dose is about 100 micrograms (0.0001 g, roughly 1/5000 of an aspirin pill), yet it lasts 8 to 12 hours. Normally serotonin taps its 5-HT2A receptor for an instant and the neuron fires once. LSD has the same two-ring core, so it fits the same lock, and then a lid on the receptor (extracellular loop 2, seen in a 2017 crystal structure) snaps shut over it. Stuck for hours, the neuron fires nonstop. 5-HT2A is packed in the cortex, so the cortex gets loud: fMRI shows brain networks that normally stay in their lane all cross-talking (linked to hallucinations), while the default mode network, the brain's "self" network, falls apart (linked to ego dissolution). Risks are real: panic, and psychosis in vulnerable people. Science explainer, not advice. Sound on 🔊

## How does a light bulb make light? (out/bulb.mp4)
Title: How a light bulb ACTUALLY makes light 💡🤯 #shorts
Description:
A 10 W LED fires about 10 quintillion photons every second. Inside its 1 mm chip sit two crystals: one with extra electrons (N), one with "holes", missing electrons (P). Switch on 3 volts and they're pushed to the edge, where an electron falls into a hole and its extra energy flies out as one photon. The bigger the fall (the energy gap), the bluer the light: blue was so hard to make it won the 2014 Nobel Prize. White LEDs are a blue chip under a yellow phosphor coat. Old bulbs heated a wire to ~2,500°C and turned ~90% of the power into heat; LEDs give the same light with 75% less power and last up to 25x longer. Sound on 🔊 every flash of light is a note.

## How does anesthesia switch you off? (out/anesthesia.mp4)
Title: How anesthesia ACTUALLY switches your brain off 💉😵 #shorts
Description:
Propofol, the milky drug nicknamed "milk of amnesia", knocks you out in about 30 seconds. Awake, your neurons fire nonstop above their firing threshold. GABA is the brain's brake: it opens a door in the GABA-A receptor that lets chloride in, calming the neuron. Propofol jams that door wide open, chloride floods in, and the neuron sinks below threshold into silence. Across billions of neurons, the EEG turns from fast chatter into huge slow waves, and the thalamus stops relaying signals to the cortex. It isn't sleep: a nudge wakes you from sleep, surgery doesn't wake you from this. It's closer to a reversible coma. Stop the drip, the drug washes out, and you're awake in minutes. Sound on 🔊 the music goes under with you.

## PIXEL QUIZ: how screens work (out/pixelquiz.mp4)
Title: Your screen has NO yellow. Can you beat this quiz? 📱🤯 #shorts
Description:
3 levels, 3 seconds to answer each. LEVEL 1: your screen has no yellow, so what makes it? Zoom in: every pixel is just 3 tiny lights, red, green and blue. Red + green look yellow because the lights are too small to see apart, so your eye blurs them together. LEVEL 2: what makes white? All 3 on. Black is all 3 off (on an OLED the pixel really switches off). Each light has 256 levels, which gives 16.7 million colours. LEVEL 3: how many lights in a phone screen?
