## How does a chameleon change color? (out/chameleon.mp4)
Title: How does a chameleon change color? 🦎 #shorts
Description:
It's not just paint, it's light. Tiny crystals in its skin reflect blue when calm (blue + yellow pigment = green) and spread apart to reflect red when excited. Sound on: every colour is a chord.
