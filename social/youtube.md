## How does a chameleon change color? · Kurzgesagt-style pixel remake (out/chameleon-k.mp4)
Title: How does a chameleon REALLY change color? 🦎🌈 #shorts
Description:
White light is a mix of all colours. Inside a chameleon's skin, rows of tiny crystals bounce back only one colour: the gap between the rows picks it. Small gap = blue, and blue + its yellow pigment = green. When it gets excited, the cell stretches, the gap grows, and the light turns red. Sound on: every light wave is a note 🎧

## How does an air fryer actually work? (out/airfryer.mp4 — chunky-pixel style + v2 music; same copy works for airfryer-v2.mp4)
Title: Your air fryer doesn't fry. Here's what it really does 🍟🤯 #shorts
Description:
An air fryer is a tiny turbo oven: a heater gets up to 200°C and a fan blasts that hot air around the basket. The hot wind blows the water off the surface of the fries, and once the surface passes ~140°C the Maillard reaction turns it golden and crunchy. Sound on 🔊 every water molecule is a bloop, every golden fry is a note.

## What does LSD actually do to your brain? (out/lsd.mp4)
Title: What LSD actually does to your brain 🧠🤯 #shorts
Description:
A dose is about 100 micrograms (0.0001 g, roughly 1/5000 of an aspirin pill), yet it lasts 8 to 12 hours. Normally serotonin taps its 5-HT2A receptor for an instant and the neuron fires once. LSD has the same two-ring core, so it fits the same lock, and then a lid on the receptor (extracellular loop 2, seen in a 2017 crystal structure) snaps shut over it. Stuck for hours, the neuron fires nonstop. 5-HT2A is packed in the cortex, so the cortex gets loud: fMRI shows brain networks that normally stay in their lane all cross-talking (linked to hallucinations), while the default mode network, the brain's "self" network, falls apart (linked to ego dissolution). Risks are real: panic, and psychosis in vulnerable people. Science explainer, not advice. Sound on 🔊
#neuroscience #brain #science #howitworks #shorts
