# 007 · How does fusion power the Sun?

48 s · seamless loop · 9:16 · 60 fps · **150 BPM (hyper)** · true pixel-art 3D (space palette). Build: `npm run video:fusion` (~3 min).

Sun → cut-away to the core → plasma (protons repel) → p+p→D (+e⁺ +ν) → D+p→He-3 (+γ) → He-3+He-3→He-4 (+2p)
→ 4 protons in, 1 helium out, 0.7% lighter → E = mc² → fusion storm (drop 2) → a photon random-walks out → 8 minutes to Earth.

Music: four-on-the-floor, offbeat open hats, rolling 16th bass, supersaw chords side-chained to the kick, snare-roll
builds and two drops; every fusion is an impact, every photon bounce a note.

Facts: core ~15 million °C; ~600 million tonnes of hydrogen fused per second, ~4 million tonnes of mass → energy
per second; atomic masses 4 × 1.008 = 4.032 vs He-4 4.003 (~0.7%); light takes thousands of years (estimates
~10,000–170,000) to escape, ~8 minutes to Earth; Sun ~1.4 million km wide.
